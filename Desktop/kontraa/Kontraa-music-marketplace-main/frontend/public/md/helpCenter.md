### 1 Contributor Verification and Onboarding

#### 1.1 Applying as a Contributor

Q. How do I apply to become a contributor on Kontraa?

- Click on the “Become a Contributor” button on the homepage
- Fill out the application form with your personal details, portfolio links, and any required documentation to showcase your previous work.
- Submit the form and wait for the verification process to be completed.

#### 1.2 Verification Process

Q. What is the verification process for contributors?

- After submitting your application, our team will review your details and portfolio to ensure you meet our quality and compliance standards.
- Verification may take up to [Insert Time Frame]. You will receive an email notification regarding the status of your application.
- Once verified, you will gain access to the Contributor Dashboard, where you can manage your content, rights, and monetization options.

#### 1.3 Access to the Contributor Dashboard

Q. What can I do in the Contributor Dashboard?

- Manage Content: Upload, edit, and organize your music, videos, photos, sound effects, sound banks, and presets.
- Rights Management: Submit your content to the Content ID system, manage licensing information, and monitor usage.
- Monetization Program: Apply for the Kontraa Monetization Program to start earning from your contributions.

### 2 Managing Your Content

#### 2.1 Contributor Verification and Onboarding

Q. How do I upload my content on Kontraa?

- Go to the 'Manage Content' section in your Contributor Dashboard.
- Click on “Upload Content” and select the type of content (Music, Videos, Photos, Sound Effects, Sound Bank, or Presets).
- Fill in the required details such as title, description, tags, and licensing information.
- Submit your content for review. Once approved, it will be available on the platform.

#### 2.2 Editing Content

Q. How do I edit my uploaded content?

- Navigate to the 'Manage Content' section.
- Click on the content you wish to edit, make the necessary changes, and save

#### 2.3 Rights Management and Content ID

Q. How do I register my content with Content ID?

- Go to the 'Rights Management' section in your dashboard.
- Select the content you want to register and click on 'Submit to Content ID'.
- Provide accurate metadata and ownership information to ensure proper recognition and monetization.

### 3 Kontraa Monetization Program

#### 3.1 Overview of the Monetization Program

Q. What is the Kontraa Monetization Program?

- The Kontraa Monetization Program allows verified contributors to earn revenue from their content through licensing, direct sales, and more.
- Contributors gain access to additional tools for reporting, insights, and compliance on licensed works and recordings.

#### 3.2 How to Apply for the Monetization Program

Q. How do I apply for the Monetization Program?

- Go to the 'Monetization' section in your Contributor Dashboard.
- Click on “Apply for Monetization” and fill out the required information.
- Your application will be reviewed, and upon approval, you will be able to start monetizing your content.

#### 3.3 Monetization Features

Q. What features are available in the Monetization Program?

- Licensed Content Access: Easily manage and license your content for various uses.
- Improved Reporting: Get detailed insights into your content’s performance, including usage data, earnings, and royalty reports.
- Automated Operations: Optimize your workflow with automated data management and reporting tools.
- Soundmouse Integration: Access Soundmouse’s global cue sheet network for enhanced tracking and compliance.

#### 3.4 Platform Fees and Revenue Distribution

Q. What are the platform fees for contributors?

- Kontraa charges a 15% service fee on the sale of content such as music, videos, photos, sound effects, sound banks, and presets.
- For distribution and publishing services, Kontraa charges a 20% service fee.
- The remaining 80-85% of the revenue goes directly to the contributor, with no hidden fees and no annual subscription plans.
